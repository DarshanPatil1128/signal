The model

Features. Text columns become one-hot indicators; numeric columns are standardized so their weights are comparable. ID-like columns are ignored.

Training. Logistic regression fitted by batch gradient descent with L2 regularization, on a random training split. Every chart reports on leads the model never saw.

Evaluation. AUC measures how often a converter outranks a non-converter. Cumulative gains shows the business version: how many conversions you find if reps only call the top slice.

Explanations. Each lead’s reasons are the features contributing most to its score (weight × value), which is exact for a linear model.

Upload any CSV with a 0/1 converted column. Sample columns: lead_id, source, industry, company_size, title_level, pages_viewed, email_opens, demo_requested, days_since_last_touch, converted. Sample data is synthetic.
